A product declares its stage in one file. If it declares nothing it is treated as production, so forgetting never makes a product look safer than it is.

About 2 minutes. The stage is declared by the product in a file called .governance.yml, or a marker in the README, or a platform-set repository variable. Each guardrail says which stage it starts at and then applies to every later stage. The strictest declaration wins, a pull request can raise the stage but never lower it, and raising it re-checks the whole repository, so promotion is a readiness check. The numbers are from the example rules in the proof of concept; your governance teams will set the real ones.
