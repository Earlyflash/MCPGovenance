Built to be trusted

Fails closed

A crashed or missing check blocks. A broken gate never looks like a pass.

Policy out of reach

Rules live in the governance repo. A pull request cannot loosen the rules that judge it.

Exceptions are visible

Waivers are dated, capped at 90 days and shown in every comment. Secrets can never be waived.

Humans read, machines enforce

Every finding links to the plain-language policy and to the exact rule that produced it.

About 1 minute. These are the properties that make people trust a gate. If a required check crashes, the pull request fails rather than passing silently. Policy is held in the governance repo, not in the repo being judged. Waivers live in the product repo but are dated, narrow and visible. Finally, every finding links back to the policy people actually read.
